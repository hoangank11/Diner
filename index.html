About the shop

We are a small family kitchen at the end of a quiet alley. The broth goes on the stove at three in the morning, the bread arrives warm from the bakery next door at half past five, and the first bowl is on the table at six.

Ten dishes, done properly. Nothing frozen, nothing rushed. Come hungry, or order ahead and pick it up on your way to work.
